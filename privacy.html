Privacy

Effective 2026-10-21.

Northclerk runs a front desk that answers a business's customers by email, books appointments and sends the business a daily summary. Software drafts the replies, and while the service is new a person at Northclerk reads each message and approves each reply before it goes out. Website chat and texting are not switched on yet. This page says what we collect, why, and who sees it.

This website

The interactive demo is paused and has no message input. This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers. Our web host (GitHub, Inc.) receives visitors' IP addresses and may log them for security. The Get started form opens a draft in your email app containing your business name, trade, ZIP code, email, phone, number of trucks and what you're interested in. Opening the draft does not send it. If you choose to send it, we receive that information and anything else you add, and keep it to reply to your inquiry and, if you sign up, to set up your account. Please do not send records, passwords or account details yet.

Businesses that use Northclerk

When a business signs up, we collect what it sends us to set up its front desk: business name, contact name, email address, phone number, services and prices, hours, service area and booking rules. We use it only to run that business's front desk and to email the owner about the account.

Paperwork and cleanup jobs

If you hire us for a data cleanup, or for trucking records once that opens, we collect the files you send (for cleanup, the export or workbook; for trucking, fuel and mileage exports, truck labels, filing dates and driver-document expiry dates). We use them only to do that job and return the results to you.

People who contact a business that uses Northclerk

When you email a business that uses Northclerk, or text it once texting is connected, we receive what you send: your name, phone number, address, the job you describe and your messages. We use it only to answer you, book or change your appointment, and tell that business about your request. The business you contacted sees your messages and bookings. While the service is new, a person at Northclerk also reads them.

Text messages

Northclerk does not send text messages yet.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Who else handles the data

We use these service providers to run Northclerk: Google (Gmail and Google Calendar), GitHub, Inc. (website hosting), PayPal (payments for paid work) and Anthropic (the AI model that reads messages and drafts replies). Each of them handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. We share only what is needed to run the front desk or do the job you hired us for. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation and booking records while the business uses Northclerk, and delete them within 90 days after it leaves, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. We keep sign-up emails and trucking-list entries until you ask us to delete them.
