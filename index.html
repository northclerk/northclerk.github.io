FAQ

Short answers to what owners ask first.

Why is the front desk free?

It costs us little to run, and it shows you how we work. We charge for websites, cleanup and records work. It covers up to 100 conversations a month, and we will tell you before anything changes.

Will it make up prices?

No. It quotes only from your price list. Anything else goes to you.

Do I have to change my email or phone number?

No. You keep your email, phone number and website, and forward your business email to your Northclerk inbox. Replies to customers come from that inbox, which is a Gmail address.

Can I see what it told my customers?

Yes. Every conversation is in your evening summary, and we send the full thread whenever you ask.

What if I want to stop?

Tell us and we stop. No contract.
